# Code of conduct

Snipe Bridge contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the software.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's secrets or private data are not acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome when it addresses the work rather than the person.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.
